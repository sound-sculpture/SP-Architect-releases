# Privacy

SP Architect does not collect, store or transmit personal data.

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
- No network requests are made by the app.
- Projects, samples and preferences stay on your computer (or in the app's sandbox on iPad).
- Files are only read or written where you explicitly choose them (open/save dialogs, card folders).

If you contact support, only what you choose to send (screenshots, logs, project files) is shared.
