SP-303 · SP-404 OG · SP-404 SX/A · SP-404 MK2 · SP-555

Five SPs. One app.

SP Architect lets you prepare your beat sets for the five iconic Roland samplers (SP-303, SP-404 OG, SP-404 SX/A, SP-404 MK2 and SP-555). Start from a blank project, or open a card the machine already wrote: the app understands it, recovers the samples and settings, and lets you rearrange everything. On the SP-404 MK2 you can also plug the machine in over USB and work directly inside its projects, without going through the card. Then write the card, a ZIP or a project file — “one app to rule them all”, for these five anyway — and go play.

THE MACHINES

One card per machine

Pick your SP at startup: the interface and the settings adapt. SP-202, SP-606 and SP-808 are not supported.

SP-303

32 pads (8 per bank) on SmartMedia. The card is rewritten as a whole: no merging or shifting.

An original card, read back in the editor.

SP-404 OG

96 pads (12 across 8 banks) on CompactFlash. The card's GATE / LOOP / REVERSE settings are kept.

12 pads per bank, settings per pad.

SP-404 SX / A

120 pads (12 across 10 banks) on SD, one audio file per pad. Patterns are not supported yet.

120 pads across ten banks.

SP-404 MK2

160 pads (16 across 10 banks) on SD, with the USB link, patterns and screens. See the MK2 section.

Live project: per-pad tempo, bus, groups.

SP-555

128 slots (16 across 8 banks) on CompactFlash; pads already on the card are recovered.

A real card, 95 of 128 pads.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, always the same

1 · Start blank, or from a card

An empty set if you want to build everything. Or a card (SD, CompactFlash, SmartMedia), or a folder already copied to disk: the app recognises the machine, reads the samples and puts the pads back in order.

2 · Drop, arrange

Drop your files — several at once if you like: they fill the free pads in order. Then move them with the mouse until the set feels right.

3 · Set, listen

Level, pan, pitch, loop, filter, envelope, tempo… all in one panel per pad. Keyboard preview, and the card gauge tells you how much room is left.

4 · Write

To the card (ready to put in the machine), as a ZIP, or as a project file. Nothing is written until you click, and every write shows its progress.

THE DETAILS THAT SAVE TIME

Built to move fast

Many files at once

Drop 10, 20, 50 files on the grid: they land on the free pads in order, instead of one by one.

A pad to the desktop or your DAW

Drag a pad to the Finder, the desktop, an Ableton track or any other DAW: you get the WAV back. Handy to reuse a sample or work on it elsewhere.

Tempo and key detected

BPM and key are detected on import and shown on every pad. MK2, SX/A, OG and 555 store that tempo in their pad table, so it appears on the machine too. The SP-303 has no tempo field.

Gauge and safeguards

The gauge shows what is left on the card and warns when the set exceeds its capacity (the 1 GB limit of a 404 OG CompactFlash, for instance).

Light or dark, EN / FR / ES

Two themes, three languages. The interface follows the machine: each module has its own colour.

Export

One button to get the set out: a ZIP ready to copy, a direct USB send (MK2), or a write to the card. The target's patterns and screens are preserved.

Your card stays readable

When the card already holds pads, the app shows both sides before touching a byte: used pads, free slots, conflicts, files the machine cannot see.

  • Replace, merge, keep the card, shift or integrate;
  • files the app does not recognise are never deleted;
  • every write shows its progress and is verified.

Analysis before writing, bank by bank.

The guide lives in the menu

No PDF to hunt down: each machine's guide opens from the ☰ menu, with the card capacity, the expected file tree, every pad setting and the import procedure on the machine.

Each machine's guide, inside the app.

The whole pad

Rename, replace, move a sample, chop, fade, normalise, trim — without leaving the pad window. And the interface fits the machine: the MK2 shows all its settings, an SP-555 or SP-303 only theirs.

SP-404 MK2

The MK2 goes further: USB, patterns, screens

Local

The app works on its own: nothing is sent to the machine while you edit. And everything stays exportable afterwards — ZIP, project, or a direct write to the SD card.

Live

The app works directly on the project inside the MK2, connected over USB: you read what is on the machine, you edit, and each setting goes into its project.

Internal storage

Browse the internal storage and the SD card, open a folder, rename a project, move a file, or load a project into the set — without leaving the app.

Patterns

Read the project's patterns, edit them on a grid, write them back to the machine. MIDI export, Bounce to a pad and MULTIPAD to a bank, with pitch, sync, volume and pan.

Chop

Cut a sample into 2, 4, 8 slices — evenly, by level or on transients. Audition each slice from the keyboard, then create the pads in one go, on free slots or ones you pick.

Screens

Startup animation and screen saver: six images, drawn pixel by pixel or imported from a picture (with cropping). Preview the animation, keep sets in a library and apply them to several projects, export PNG.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

What works, what still needs checking

Always back up a card's contents before a first test. The app never deletes files it does not recognise, but a write is still a write.

  • Beta. SP-303, SP-404 OG, SP-555 and MK2 (USB) have been tested on hardware. Still to confirm: SD-card writing on MK2 and SX/A, and very large cards.
  • SP-404 SX/A: samples and settings handled, patterns not yet.
  • iPad: editor / librarian only, no USB link.
  • Windows: MK2 USB link available through the serial bridge, as on macOS; the executable is unsigned.
  • Conversion: preparing a long sample takes time (computation, not a copy). The window shows the remaining time.

FAQ

Short answers

Windows shows a warning

That is normal: the app has no publisher certificate. Click More info → Run anyway.

Should I back up my card?

Yes, always, before a first test. The app never deletes what it does not recognise, but a write is still a write.

Local or live?

Local: the app works alone, then you export (card, ZIP, project).
Live: it works directly on the project inside the MK2, connected over USB.

Where is the source code?

It is not published. The app is free to use as a binary; redistribution or reuse of the code requires written permission.

Can the iPad drive the MK2?

No: iPadOS does not allow serial access. The iPad is an editor and librarian, without the USB link.

Does it work offline?

Yes, entirely. No account, no server, no analytics.

The MK2 is not detected

Quit the other SP Architect instances: only one can hold the USB port (/dev/cu.usbmodem…). Also check the cable and that the machine is on.
